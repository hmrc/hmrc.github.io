## Motivation

By applying internal and external standards, we help ensure consistency across our estate, improve quality and increase interoperability. We must also understand and meet all applicable legal standards.

<hr class="govuk-section-break--l govuk-section-break--visible">

## Applying this principle

- Understand what internal standards there are that apply to your service -- for example, based on the tools, technology or platform you are using, or the area you work within.
- Where internal standards don't exist or could be improved, identify how to engage with the relevant communities to contribute back to them and improve engineering quality in the organisation.
- Identify any applicable external standards and best practices there are for the tools and technology you are using.
- Be aware of how legal standards such as GDPR and accessibility legislation affect your service, and ensure these are considered throughout the lifetime of the service -- they are not a "one off" consideration.
- Engage and inform internal processes that ensure compliance with legal requirements, such as Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) and Security Risk Assessments (SRAs).
